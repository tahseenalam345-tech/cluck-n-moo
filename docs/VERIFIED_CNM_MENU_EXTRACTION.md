## 19. CONFLICTS_AND_UNCLEAR_DETAILS

### Conflict 1: Citrus Honey Crunch vs Lemon Honey Crunch vs `citrus_honey_crush`
- **IMG-2 (Photo Board):** Spelled and labeled as **"Citrus Honey Crunch [590 PKR]"**.
- **IMG-3 (Text Menu):** Spelled and labeled as **"LEMON HONEY CRUNCH 590"**, with description *"Crispy chicken, lemon honey sauce, cheese and ranch, in buns"*.
- **Cloudinary Asset:** Filename is `citrus_honey_crush_rcslnq`.
- **Recommendation:** Display as **"Citrus Honey Crunch"** (or subtitle "Lemon Honey Crunch") to preserve customer recognition, and map to `citrus_honey_crush_rcslnq`.

### Conflict 2: Mushroom n Swiss vs Mushroom n Cheese
- **IMG-2 (Photo Board):** Labeled as **"Mushroom n Swiss [740 PKR]"**.
- **IMG-3 (Text Menu):** Labeled as **"MUSHROOM N CHEESE 740"**, with description *"Thick patty, in creamy mushroom sauce and melted cheese"*.
- **Cloudinary Asset:** Filename is `mushroom_n_swiss_sgd3ct`.
- **Recommendation:** Use **"Mushroom n Swiss"** as the primary title because it matches both the visual board and the official Cloudinary asset, with subtitle referencing melted cheese.

### Conflict 3: Smashin' Cluck vs Smashed Cluck
- **IMG-2 (Photo Board):** Spelled **"Smashin' Cluck [490 PKR]"**.
- **IMG-3 (Text Menu):** Spelled **"SMASHED CLUCK 490"**, with description *"Smashed patty, caramelized onions, cheese and sauce, in buns"*.
- **Cloudinary Asset:** Filename is `smashin_cluck_zcyqml`.
- **Recommendation:** Use **"Smashin' Cluck"** as canonical title to match Cloudinary asset and photo board.

### Conflict 4: Stuffed Calzone Flavour Spelling ("Creamy Alredo")
- **IMG-1 (Deals Board):** Reads *"Flaming Tikka / Creamy Alredo 950 PKR Each"*. The word *"Alredo"* is a visible typo on the printed board for *"Alfredo"*.
- **Recommendation:** Use **"Creamy Alfredo"** in data structures, noting the menu board typo.

### Conflict 5: Extra Patty Pricing `200/250`
- **IMG-3 (Add-Ons):** Shows `EXTRA PATTY 200/250`.
- **Ambiguity:** Does `200` apply to Chicken Patty and `250` to Beef Patty? Or is it Single vs Double?
- **Recommendation:** Model as two separate modifiers: `Extra Chicken Patty (+200 PKR)` and `Extra Beef Patty (+250 PKR)` pending final restaurant confirmation.

### Conflict 6: Fried Chicken 1PC vs 3PC
- **IMG-3 (Appetizers):** Shows `1PC/3PC FRIED CHICKEN 210/570`.
- **Detail:** Single piece is 210 PKR; 3 pieces is 570 PKR (saving 60 PKR).
- **Recommendation:** Model as product `Fried Chicken` with variants `1 Piece (210 PKR)` and `3 Pieces (570 PKR)`.
